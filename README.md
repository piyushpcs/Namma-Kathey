# Namma Kathey (ನಮ್ಮ ಕಥೆ) 🦁

**Regional Hero Storybook — Inspiring the next generation with stories of bravery, one district at a time.**

---

## 🌟 The Vision
Typically every district has a "Local Legend" or "Historical Hero" (e.g., Sangolli Rayanna, Kittur Chennamma). Children know about global superheroes but don't know the "Bravery Stories" from their own district. **Namma Kathey** is an interactive history app for children that tells stories of local freedom fighters, poets, and social reformers from every district of Karnataka using simple language and modern illustrations.

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **📖 Illustrated Storybook**: A swipe-based storybook experience using `ViewPager2`, designed to keep children engaged.
*   **🔊 Text-To-Speech (TTS)**: Media-rich experience that reads stories aloud, promoting linguistic heritage in both **Kannada and English**.
*   **🧠 Hero Quiz & Heritage Badges**: Answer questions to earn "Heritage Badges" (Gold, Silver, Bronze) saved in your profile.
*   **🗺️ District Map**: Tap on a district to explore its unique heroes and their impact on history.
*   **📍 Statue Finder**: Discover the location of the nearest memorial or statue of the hero you're learning about.
*   **🌓 Linguistic Heritage**: Easy toggle between Kannada and English to promote reading in the mother tongue.

## 🎨 Design & UI
The app features a **modern, vibrant, and child-friendly UI**. We moved away from dated designs to a clean, "Off-White and Mustard" brand theme with pastel discovery cards and interactive elements that ensure high user retention.

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **Layouts**: Android XML (Material Design 3)
- **Database**: Local JSON file for stories and hero metadata.
- **Components**: ViewPager2, RecyclerView, SharedPreferences (for Badge persistence), and Google Maps integration.
- **Media**: Android Text-To-Speech (TTS) Engine.
